01 / About

Business first. Technology with purpose.

My career has centered on helping people navigate complicated, deadline-driven processes—from mortgage lending and documentation through relationship management, team leadership, operational problem-solving and customer service.

That perspective shaped how I approach technology. A useful system must fit the people using it, move accurate information where it needs to go, and produce results that can actually be tested.

In recent projects, I have directed AI-assisted implementation of websites, connected CRM workflows, databases, intake forms, alerts, customer communications, and calculation tools. My involvement spans requirements, integration decisions, testing, issue identification and iteration—not simply generating a page with a prompt.

I'm a practical and curious learner, comfortable working across business and technical teams and getting familiar with unfamiliar tools.

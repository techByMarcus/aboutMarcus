## About Me

I'm a mortgage broker and real estate investor with more than 20 years of experience across residential mortgage lending, real estate finance, client operations, compliance, and property investing.

I've worked with homebuyers, homeowners, and real estate investors through different lending environments and market cycles. I've also bought, held, renovated, and sold property myself, which gives me a different perspective when helping borrowers evaluate financing decisions.

I don't look at a mortgage as simply a transaction. I look at the full picture — the property, financing structure, risk, long-term objective, and what the borrower is ultimately trying to accomplish.

Today, I combine that experience with modern technology, AI, data, automation, and continued study in cybersecurity and risk management to operate more efficiently and make better-informed decisions.
